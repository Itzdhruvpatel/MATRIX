          Matrix Score

          A simple 0 to 100 rating for every actively traded stock, based on how its price behaved over the past year. Higher is better.

          80 to 100Strong 60 to 79Good 40 to 59Average 20 to 39Weak 0 to 19Very weak
          What goes into the score?
          • Momentum (30%): has the price been rising over the past year?
          • Trend (20%): is it above its usual price over the last 200 days?
          • Calm (20%): does the price move gently, or swing wildly?
          • Worst fall (20%): how far did it drop from its high in the past year?
          • Easy to trade (10%): is there enough buying and selling every day?

          Each part compares the stock with every other stock. The score looks at the past only. It is not a buy or sell signal. Company profits, debt and news will be added later.
